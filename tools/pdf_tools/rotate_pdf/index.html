How to Use

  1. Upload your PDF using Drag & Drop or Browse PDF.
  2. Choose All Pages or Selected Pages.
  3. If you select Selected Pages, enter pages like: 1,3,5-8,12
  4. Select your desired rotation angle.
  5. Click Rotate PDF.
  6. Download your rotated PDF.
