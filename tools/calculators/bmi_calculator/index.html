What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple calculation that estimates whether your weight is healthy for your height. It is widely used by healthcare professionals as a screening tool.

Important Note

BMI does not directly measure body fat and may not be accurate for athletes, children, pregnant women, or older adults. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

Why do we ask for gender?

Gender helps estimate ideal body weight and allows future health calculations.

Can I use feet and inches?

Yes. This calculator supports Centimeters, Meters, and Feet & Inches.
