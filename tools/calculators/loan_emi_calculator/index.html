About EMI Calculator

An EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) Calculator helps you estimate the fixed monthly payment required to repay a loan over a selected period at a specified interest rate.

This calculator uses the standard banking EMI formula, making it suitable for personal loans, home loans, car loans, education loans, and many other installment-based loans.

Simply enter the loan amount, annual interest rate, repayment period, and optional extra payments to view detailed repayment information instantly.

Formula Used

Monthly EMI Formula

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)N ÷ ((1 + R)N − 1)


P = Loan Amount

R = Monthly Interest Rate

N = Total Number of Monthly Payments

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay your loan. It includes both principal and interest.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the standard banking formula based on your loan amount, annual interest rate, and repayment period.

Can I make extra monthly payments?

Yes. This calculator allows you to enter an optional monthly extra payment to estimate the total additional amount paid during the loan period.

Does this calculator work for all types of loans?

Yes. It can be used for personal loans, home loans, car loans, education loans, business loans, and most installment-based loans.
