About BMR Calculator

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the number of calories your body needs every day to perform essential life-sustaining functions such as breathing, blood circulation, maintaining body temperature, and supporting organ function while at complete rest.

This Toolversehub BMR Calculator uses the scientifically recognized Mifflin-St Jeor Equation, which is considered one of the most accurate formulas for estimating daily calorie requirements for healthy adults.

After calculating your BMR, the calculator estimates your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE), recommended calorie intake for weight loss or weight gain, Body Mass Index (BMI), healthy weight range, recommended daily water intake, and daily protein and fat requirements.

Formula Used

Male

BMR = (10 × Weight in kg) + (6.25 × Height in cm) − (5 × Age) + 5


Female

BMR = (10 × Weight in kg) + (6.25 × Height in cm) − (5 × Age) − 161


Activity Multipliers

Sedentary = 1.20

Light Exercise = 1.375

Moderate Exercise = 1.55

Very Active = 1.725

Athlete = 1.90

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the number of calories your body burns at complete rest to keep your organs functioning.

Is this calculator accurate?

Yes. Toolversehub uses the Mifflin-St Jeor Equation, one of the most accurate and widely recommended formulas for estimating BMR.

What is TDEE?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) estimates how many calories you burn in an entire day based on your activity level.

Can I use this calculator for weight loss?

Yes. The calculator estimates calorie targets for both weight loss and weight gain based on your maintenance calories.
