About GPA Calculator

A Grade Point Average (GPA) is a numerical representation of a student's academic performance. It is calculated by multiplying the grade points earned in each subject by its credit hours, adding those values together, and dividing the result by the total credit hours.

This GPA Calculator follows the internationally recognized 4.0 grading scale and is suitable for most colleges and universities around the world.

You can add or remove subjects, enter credit hours, choose your letter grades, and instantly calculate your GPA with accurate results.

Formula Used

Grade Point Average (GPA)

GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Credit Hours


Example

If a subject has 3 credit hours and the student earns an A (4.00), then:

3 × 4.00 = 12 Grade Points

The GPA is calculated by adding all grade points and dividing them by the total credit hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GPA?

GPA (Grade Point Average) is a numerical value that represents your overall academic performance based on the grades and credit hours of your subjects.

How is GPA calculated?

Each subject's grade point is multiplied by its credit hours. The sum of all grade points is then divided by the total credit hours.

Which grading scale does this calculator use?

This calculator uses the International 4.0 GPA grading scale including A+, A, A-, B+, B, B-, C+, C, C-, D+, D and F grades.

Can I add more than five subjects?

Yes. Click the + Add Subject button to add as many subjects as you need.
