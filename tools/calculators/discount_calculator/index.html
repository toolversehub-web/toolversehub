What is a Discount Calculator?

Whether you're shopping online, comparing deals, or calculating sale prices in a store, this calculator gives accurate results instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a percentage discount calculated?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100. Subtract the result from the original price.

What is a fixed discount?

A fixed discount subtracts a specific amount instead of a percentage.

Can I calculate tax?

Yes. Simply enter the tax percentage and the calculator will include it in the final price.
