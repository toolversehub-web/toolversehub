About Age Calculator

Toolversehub Age Calculator helps you calculate your exact age from your date of birth using today's date automatically. It provides accurate results in years, months and days while also showing your birth day, zodiac sign, leap year status, next birthday and birthday countdown.

Age Calculation Formula

Age = Current Date − Date of Birth

The calculator automatically considers leap years, different month lengths and the current date to provide accurate results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this calculator work?

It compares your date of birth with today's date and calculates your exact age.

Does it support leap years?

Yes. Leap years are detected automatically to ensure accurate calculations.

Can I calculate my next birthday?

Yes. The calculator automatically displays your next birthday along with the remaining countdown.
